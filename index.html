About

I am a young researcher based at Bangladesh Agricultural University, Mymensingh. My work centres on complete genome sequencing and characterisation of animal and human viruses from Bangladesh, including hepatitis B, lumpy skin disease virus, fowlpox, chicken anemia virus and H5N1 avian influenza, complemented by in-silico drug and vaccine design. I currently contribute to a project on an inactivated vaccine for lumpy skin disease virus developed from a local isolate.
